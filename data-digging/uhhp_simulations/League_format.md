ROSTERS
Rosters must be comprised of up to 24 active players:
 
Active roster
• 2 Goalies
• 2 Centres
• 3 Wingers
• 4 Forwards (C or W)
• 4 Defense
These are the minimum requirements to qualify for play
 
Reserve roster
• no limit
 
In addition, there is an area (injury roster) where we house
• Rookies
• Future draft picks
• Cap hits
 
Rookies
There is no limit on rookie players.  Only rookies from the entry draft (team selected or acquired via trade from another owner) can be classified as a rookie (i.e., Macklin C).  Players on a Rookie Contract can not be dressed / accumulate points.  An owner can choose to move a player from their Rookie Roster to their active roster at any time, at minimum salary (2$) and for maximum term (3 years).  A player can be left as a rookie for 3 years maximum. Once a player is moved to an active roster, they cannot be sent down or reclassified as a rookie. A player can be dropped from this roster at any time (after which they go into the free agent pool and are subject to the waiver/UFA process).  A rookie will automatically become a UFA if they are unsigned after the 3 year maximum.

SALARY
Each team’s annual salary cap at season start must be at or below 100 units
Minimum annual salary for a player = 2 units
A player’s annual salary must be a whole number (i.e., no half units)
There is no maximum on any one player’s annual salary
 
TERM
The minimum length of a player’s contract = 1 year
The maximum length of a player’s contract = 3 years
 
After the season starts, an owner may go over and above 100 in cap.  If on playoff start date, the owner is still over 100, they will be subject to a luxury tax (real dollars) as per this formula:
​​1-5 over = 2x overage
​​6-25 over = 3x overage
​​26-50 over = 4x overage
​​> 50 = 5x overage
 
FREE AGENCY
Any player under the age of 27 on June 30 of the draft day’s year whose contract has expired is a Restricted Free Agent (RFA).
 
Any player at or above the age of 27 on June 30 of the draft day’s year whose contract has expired is an Unrestricted Free Agent (UFA).
 
DRAFT DAY
This year, on or near draft day, we will go through the following stages:
 
Buyouts
Each team has the option to buyout roster players they have under contract.  There is no limit to the number of players an owner may buyout.  The buyout price (in real cash) is a player’s annual salary multiplied by the number of years remaining on their contract.  There is no cap hit for these buyouts if the player is on a 1 year deal.  If the player is on a 2 year deal, a cap hit will be placed on an owner’s roster equalling half the player’s annual salary (rounded up) for one year.  Whole caphits are tradeable
 
We will ask for buyouts a few days in advance to expedite the draft process.

This stage will go 1 round.  Only players that were drafted in the 2024 NHL Entry Draft are eligible to be drafted here.
 
Superstar Round
This stage will go owner by owner for 1 round.  When it is an owner’s turn, they will nominate any available player (UFA or RFA) they would like to go up for auction.  The auction of that player will immediately follow.  
 
Note: All players signed during (any round of) the draft are on 3 year contracts.
 
UFA Nominations
This stage will go owner by owner for 2 rounds.  When it is an owner’s turn, they will nominate an available player they would like to go up for auction.  The auction of that player will immediately follow.  
 
RFA Poaching
This stage will go owner by owner for an unlimited number of rounds (until all owners tap out or the list of RFAs is exhausted).  When it is an owner’s turn, they can nominate an RFA player from any other owner’s team they would like to go up for auction.  The auction of that player will immediately follow.  The owner of that player will have the right to match the highest bid.  If they decline, the rights and ownership of that player will be transferred to the highest bidder.
 
Free Agent Auction Bidding (Online bidding)
After the draft, we will open the league to the online auction process (through CBSSportsline).  The amount you have to bid will be adjusted on how much salary cap space you have after the draft.  Players available online can be signed to one year deals ONLY!  The online auction process will run once per day for the first few days after the draft (prior to the season beginning).  After the season starts, the online process will run once per week.
 
NOTE: Owners do not have to fill their roster at the draft. They do need to have their minimum rosters filled in time for Week 1 start at or below 100$ however.  
 
 
INJURIES
The league does have a Long Term Injury (LTI) process.  If a player has been injured for 45 days, they are considered LTI eligible.  The owner has the option to release this player at a cost of their current year salary (in real cash) and no cap hit.  The player would immediately become a free agent.  The dropping owner is blacked out from picking that player up for 45 days.
 
NOTE: If a player is injured during the offseason, the 45 days begins at the beginning of the regular season.
 
 
IN SEASON BUYOUTS
An owner always has the option to buyout roster players they have under contract.  There is no limit to the number of players an owner may buyout.  The buyout price (in real cash) is a player’s annual salary multiplied by the number of years remaining on their contract.  The cap hit for these buyouts if the player is on a 1 year deal is half of the player’s annual salary (rounded up) for the rest of the current year.  If the player is on a 2 or 3 year deal, the cap hit will be half the player’s annual salary (rounded up) for rest of the current year plus 1 more year.  
 
 
RETIREMENT / KHL EXEMPTION
If an owner owns a player that retires from the NHL or goes to an overseas (AHL is not overseas) league, they have the ability to freely release that player (and their accompanying salary and term). If an owner wants to keep a player who is deciding on their future, they can at their contracted rate and term until they choose otherwise.
 
 
TRADES
Player swaps must be processed using the trade function within CBSSportsline.  They will be subject to Commissioner approval.
 
There is a trade deadline; it will be on Sunday, Feb 23, 2025. Any trades that are in the pending (i.e. agreed upon by both parties) state on or before that date still be processed according league rules.  Any other (offered but not accepted) trades will be cancelled and no new trades can be offered.  
 
 
END OF SEASON BLACKOUT
Once the championship game is over, the league will be in a transaction blackout period where no trades and/or free agent signings will be permitted.  The transaction blackout period will end near the following year's draft.
 
 
EXPANSION
Expansion must be agreed upon by the GMs.  Majority rules (7 out of 12 minimum to approve any changes)
 
 
CONTRACTION
An owner wanting to depart the league will not receive a refund of their buy in.  The Commissioner will attempt to find a suitable new owner to take over the team wholesale.  
 
 
PROCESS FOR MAKING CHANGES TO THE LEAGUE
If an owner wishes to propose a change to the league, the process is as follows: ideas are proposed to the League Competition Committee.  This committee will discuss, debate and analyze. From there, high graded ideas will be brought forward to the League of General Managers. GM’s will discuss and vote from there.
 
 
COMMISSIONER
The Commissioners rule the league.  What they say goes.  They will endeavour to be fair and equitable to all owners.
