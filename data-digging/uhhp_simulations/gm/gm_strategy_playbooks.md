# GM Strategy Playbooks

## Winning Roster Blueprint (UHHP, 2022–2024)
- Elite core
  - 2–3 elite forwards across C/W at $12–20 each; avoid allocating >25% of cap to any single player.
  - 2 elite defensemen at $10–12; plus 2 value D at $2–4 to round out the blue line.
- Goalies
  - 2 reliable starters totaling $7–12. Prefer balance over a single luxury goalie (> $7).
- Depth construction
  - 6–10 depth players at $2–5 to fill F/W/C and absorb injuries; prioritize multi-category stability and PP/TOI roles.
- Budget rails (guideline)
  - Forwards: $35–45; Defense: $22–30; Goalies: $7–12; Depth/flex: remainder.
  - Cap any single rostered bid to ≤ 20–22 unless you already have surplus value locked elsewhere (see variations).

### Tier-based blueprint (using current clusters)
- Elite density (observed in top-3 teams): 3–5 total tier-0 players across the roster, typically:
  - At least 1 elite C/W and 1 elite D; second elite D is common on winners; elite G is optional but valuable.
- Tier-2 spine: 4–7 players in tier 2 across W and D; this is a consistent separator for top finishes.
- Goalies: either 1 tier-0 plus 1 tier-2/3, or two tier-2/3; avoid tier-4/5 reliance.
- Depth: 6–10 players across tiers 3–5; aim for ≥3 in tier 3.

#### Position-by-position tier targets
- Centers (C): 1–2 in tier 0/1; 2–4 more in tiers 2–3
- Wings (W): 1–2 in tier 0/1; bulk in tiers 2–3, limited tier-5 minutes
- Defense (D): ≥1 tier-0; plus 1–2 in tiers 1–3; minimize tier-5 D ice
- Goalies (G): 2 total across tiers 0–3, avoid tier 5 if competitive window is open

### Variations and adjustments
- Cheap multi‑year signings (draft/rookie/keeper discounts)
  - If you hold 1–2 premium values at $2–4 (e.g., a 65–85 pt F or a PP1 D), reallocate savings to reinforce the other spine:
    - Have cheap elite F? Upgrade D spine (secure a second elite D) or stabilize G into the $10–12 total band.
    - Have cheap elite D? Push from 2 to 3 premium F (still honor per‑player cap share ≤ 25%).
    - Have a $2–3 starting goalie? Keep G total ≤ $8 and push savings to skater ceilings.
  - With 2+ cheap keepers, adopt a “wider top tier” approach (3–4 premiums at $10–18 rather than one mega‑star).

- Positional scarcity and league flow
  - If elite D pool is thin (or heavily contested), secure 1 anchor D early (≤ $12) then pivot to value D + heavier F spend.
  - If G market inflates, avoid chasing. Lock one safe starter ~$4–6 and accept a platoon second at $2–4.
  - In winger‑heavy inflation, emphasize elite C (faceoff/usage floor) and PP1 W with stable shot volume.

- Risk management
  - Limit exposure to injury‑prone premiums to one slot; offset with durable volume shooters or PP quarterbacks.
  - Maintain a minimum $2× remaining open slots reserve at all times; keep a late‑phase $6–8 strike buffer for last elite value.

- RFA/keeper interplay
  - Post‑RFA: match only if the player’s projected VORP supports $2×3 relative to replacement and you have slot/cap buffer.
  - Rookie/keeper pipeline lets you bias toward 2 premium D long‑term or expand to 3 elite F; choose one axis, not both.

### Targeting heuristics for the auction engine
- Hard rails
  - Max single‑player bid: 22 (unless ≥ $6 surplus value is locked via cheap multi‑year signings).
  - Enforce positional envelopes: D total $22–30, G total $7–12, F total $35–45.
  - Always preserve $2 × open slots reserve after any candidate bid.
- Soft preferences
  - Prioritize the second elite D by the midpoint of the superstar+UFA phases if you lack keeper D value.
  - If you roster zero bankable goalies by mid‑UFA phase, allocate at least $7 to lock two stable starters.

## Pre‑Stage 2 decision framework (Trade/Buyout planning)
Before entering Stage 2, each team should assess its timeline: win_now, win_next, or rebuild. Use current roster projections (FP vs replacement), contract terms, and cheap multi‑year signings to decide.

- win_now
  - Trades: package future picks or prospects to upgrade present‑year PP1 usage at C/W or secure an elite D2.
  - Buyouts: remove poor VORP‑per‑$ UFAs (including multi‑year if below thresholds) to free cap for premium upgrades; avoid buying out elite unless a clearly superior elite is affordable.
  - Goalies: ensure two stable starters (total $7–12); trade to fix if not.

- win_next
  - Trades: favor age/term and bank cheap multi‑year values; move rental vets at fair value, target players with 2–3 years of control.
  - Buyouts: prune 1‑year UFAs with poor VORP‑per‑$; keep elite anchors even if pricey; use cap hits selectively when multi‑year value is negative.
  - Aim to exit with 1–2 elite F, 1–2 elite D, and at least one cheap keeper at $2–4.

- rebuild
  - Trades: sell veterans for picks/prospects; prioritize consolidating into higher draft capital or blue‑chip keeper assets.
  - Buyouts: aggressively cut negative multi‑year value (use half‑salary cap‑hit rules) to reset cap; carry minimal luxury.
  - Goal is surplus value density (multiple $2–4 keepers) to enable a wider top tier in the following season.

Operational rails
- Use team outlook flags: win_now if current FP pct ≥ ~70th, win_next if Y2 FP pct ≥ ~70th; else rebuild.
- Enforce reserve: maintain $2 × remaining slots after any trade/buyout plan.
- Re‑evaluate after each move; stop once positional envelopes and timeline targets are satisfied.
